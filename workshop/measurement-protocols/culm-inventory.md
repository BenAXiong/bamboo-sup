# Culm inventory protocol

## Minimum useful measurements

For each candidate main culm:

1. Assign permanent ID.
2. Measure overall usable length.
3. Measure mass in current workshop condition.
4. Measure outside diameter at regular stations (initial CSV uses 500 mm spacing).
5. Record obvious cracks, open/flooded chambers and damage.
6. Record natural curvature/orientation photographically or with simple offsets when useful.
7. Optionally locate centre of mass and nodes.

## Mass

A calibrated hanging scale is simplest. A workshop steelyard can also be made from a stiff beam: suspend the culm at a fixed lever arm and slide a known 12 kg or 16 kg kettlebell on the opposite arm until balanced. Record lever-arm distances and calculate mass from moment balance. Keep suspended loads close to the ground and use closed/backup restraints.

## Precision

Early hydrostatic work benefits more from repeatable real measurements than false precision. Record the method so later calibration can estimate measurement error.
