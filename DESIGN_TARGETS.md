# Current design targets

## Primary object

A one-person bamboo stand-up paddleboard made from a minimal bundle of parallel culms with lightweight cross-members and lashings.

## Priorities

1. Adequate flotation and freeboard under design load.
2. Practical standing stability and tolerance of rider movement.
3. Low finished mass.
4. Small overall width and length.
5. Strong visual bow recurve / rocker.
6. Simple, inspectable construction.

## Bow intent

The bow should be visibly and unusually recurved — part of the object's identity — but the curve remains constrained by:
- manufacturable whole-culm curvature;
- cracking/ovalisation risk;
- useful waterline length;
- trim and bow reserve buoyancy;
- cross-member/lashing geometry;
- transport and beach handling.

The simulator should expose bow-rise and curve-start parameters rather than hard-code a single profile.

## Construction baseline

- Parallel main bamboo culms provide the primary displacement.
- Small bamboo cross-members maintain bundle geometry.
- Vine/rattan lashings are preferred for the intended craft vocabulary.
- Deck/mat is optional and must justify its weight.
- Culm taper and natural curvature should eventually be exploited rather than erased.

## Provisional numerical assumptions

These are simulator defaults only, not frozen design requirements:
- rider + carried gear: 80 kg;
- fresh water for conservative early workshop checks, with seawater selectable later;
- calm-water hydrostatics before wave/dynamic modelling.

Replace defaults with explicit design values before freezing P001.

## Explicitly not optimised yet

- racing speed;
- surf performance;
- touring cargo;
- sail compatibility;
- advanced hydrodynamic drag;
- production repeatability.
