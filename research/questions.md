# Research questions

Use this file for unresolved branches that should not silently become requirements.

## Hydrostatics / board comparison

- What displacement and reserve buoyancy do surfboards, longboards and SUPs typically provide relative to rider + board mass?
- What useful reference cases exist for very narrow SUPs or prone/paddle boards?
- At this scale, when does standing stability govern before total buoyancy?

## Bamboo geometry and mass

- What range of mass-per-length and taper occurs in the actual workshop stock?
- Can natural culm curvature be selected/oriented to reduce required fire bending?
- Is selective removal of internal material ever worthwhile after accounting for sealing, structural loss and water-ingress risk?

## Recurved bow

- How much bow rise is useful for wave handling versus primarily visual impact?
- What fraction of overall length can be recurved before useful waterline/trim degrades significantly?
- Should central and outer culms receive different bow curves?

## Construction

- Minimum cross-member count/spacing needed to prevent relative rotation and bundle deformation?
- How much mass does a woven bamboo deck add, and is it needed for footing?
- Can a folding seam be made without creating an unacceptable bending/lashing discontinuity?
- Would minimal outriggers allow a dramatically narrower main board while remaining SUP-like?
